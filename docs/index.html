Immediate Action: Taste of India (Ardmore) Ordering Guide

Recommended Order Strategy

Focus on Tandoori (Dry heat/Grilled) and Tomato/Onion bases. Avoid "Korma" or "Makhani" (Cream/Butter) to save calories.

For the Meat Eater (Paleo-ish)

  • Order: Tandoori Chicken (Half or Full)
  • Why: Grilled, high protein, no heavy sauce.
  • Stats: ~400-500 cals, 50g Protein.
  • Side: Baingan Bharta (Roasted Eggplant) - ask for minimal oil.

For the Vegan

  • Order: Chana Masala or Bhindi Masala (Okra)
  • Request: "Dairy-free/No Ghee" (usually cooked with oil, but verify).
  • Side: Tarka Dal (Yellow Lentils).
  • Carb: Roti (Whole wheat) instead of Naan (Naan usually has egg/milk).

Shared Portion Control

  • Rice: Skip the full bowl of rice. Measure out 1/2 cup each.
  • Appetizers: Skip samosas (deep fried). If hungry, ask for Kachumber Salad (cucumber/onion/tomato).

The "Wolf" Chili Drizzle (DIY)

DIY Chili Crisp
1. Heat 1/2 cup neutral oil (Avocado oil from Aldi) in a pan.
2. Add 5 cloves minced garlic + 1 shallot (minced). Fry until golden.
3. Pour hot oil over bowl containing: 3 tbsp Red Pepper Flakes, 1 tbsp Soy Sauce (or Tamari), 1 tsp Sugar/Honey.
4. Let cool. Put on everything.

Assembly Guide

  • Base: Massaged Kale (rub kale with drop of olive oil to soften).
  • Grain: 1/2 Cup Wild Rice.
  • Roasted Veg: Sweet potatoes (cubed, roasted 400°F for 25 mins).
  • Crunch: Diced apples + Almonds.
  • Finish: Balsamic Vinaigrette + Chili Drizzle.

Lime Cilantro Jalapeño Vinaigrette

Ingredients:
• 1 cup fresh cilantro
• 1 jalapeño (seeded for mild)
• Juice of 2 limes
• 1/3 cup olive oil
• 1 garlic clove
• Salt to taste
Blend all until smooth

Assembly

  • Base: 50/50 Romaine + Arugula
  • Protein: Grilled chicken with cumin & paprika (or marinated tofu)
  • Fresh Guac: Mash 2 avocados + lime juice + salt + diced jalapeño
  • Toppings: Cherry tomatoes, red onion, crushed tortilla chips
  • Dress: Lime cilantro vinaigrette (generous!)

Green Goddess Dressing (DIY Version)

Ingredients:
• 1/2 cup Greek yogurt (or cashew cream)
• 1/4 cup fresh basil
• 2 tbsp fresh chives
• Juice of 1 lemon
• 1 garlic clove
• Salt & pepper
Blend until creamy

Assembly

  • Add: Cherry tomatoes, avocado slices, blue cheese
  • Dress: Green goddess or balsamic vinaigrette

Miso Sesame Ginger Dressing

Ingredients:
• 2 tbsp white miso paste
• 1 tbsp sesame oil
• 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
• 1 tsp fresh ginger (grated)
• 1 tsp maple syrup
• 2 tbsp water
Whisk until smooth

Assembly

  • Base: Warm quinoa + fresh spinach/arugula
  • Star: Roasted mushroom mix (400°F, 20 mins with soy sauce glaze)
  • Protein: Crispy tofu cubes (press, cube, pan-fry)
  • Crunch: Cucumber ribbons, shredded carrots
  • Finish: Miso dressing + toasted sesame seeds

Hot Honey Mustard

Ingredients:
• 1/4 cup Dijon mustard
• 2 tbsp honey
• 1 tbsp hot sauce
• 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
• Pinch of cayenne
Mix and let flavors meld

Assembly

  • Fresh: Cabbage carrot slaw (shred + lime + salt)
  • Base: Bed of arugula
  • Drizzle: Hot honey mustard generously

Ardmore Shopping Intel

Aldi N Commerce St

Best Buys:

  • Organic Grass Fed Beef: Usually cheapest in town per lb.
  • Avocados: Often on "Aldi Finds" sale for <$0.60.
  • Goat Cheese: Emporium Selection logs are half the price of other stores.
  • Never Any! Brand: Antibiotic-free chicken/meats. Excellent value.

Albertsons N Commerce St

Strategic Use:

  • Vegan Specialty: Better selection of Beyond Meat/Impossible and specific vegan cheeses than Aldi.
  • Wednesdays: New weekly ads start. Look for "For U" digital coupons on the app before going.
  • Rotisserie Chickens: Good for emergency protein (remove skin for healthier option).

Walmart & Amazon

  • Walmart: Backup for produce if Aldi is out. Good for bulk rice/beans.
  • Amazon/Whole Foods: 73401 delivery is spotty for fresh food. Use Amazon for shelf-stable specialty items: Coconut Aminos, Nutritional Yeast, Specialty Hot Sauces (Son of Wolf alternatives).
